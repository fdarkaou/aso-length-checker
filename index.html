App Store ASO Length Checker

Paste your App Store Connect metadata to check every field against Apple's character limits and spot wasted keyword space. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

      Comma-separated. removes spaces after commas, duplicates and words already in the name or subtitle.

            Your inputs are kept in this browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't lose them.

            About the limits. App name and subtitle: 30 characters each. Keywords: 100 characters. Promotional text: 170. Description: 4,000. Characters are counted as user-perceived characters (emoji and accented letters count once). Always confirm in App Store Connect, which is the final authority.

            About the keyword tips. Common ASO practice: separate keywords with commas and no spaces, don't repeat a word, and don't spend keyword space on words already in your app name or subtitle. These are heuristics, not Apple rules.

            Once your metadata fits, compare it with what your competitors rank for. PeekPanda keeps a case file of dated App Store evidence for iOS apps; its keyword gap view splits searches into theirs only, both, and yours only.
